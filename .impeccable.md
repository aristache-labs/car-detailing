## Design Context

### Users
Mixed audience: private car owners and broader prospects evaluating premium detailing services.
Primary jobs-to-be-done:
- Understand service options and proof of quality quickly
- Build trust in workmanship and professionalism
- Convert into quote/contact submissions or direct phone/WhatsApp outreach

### Brand Personality
- Ultra-premium
- Precise
- Trustworthy

Emotional goals:
- Confidence before purchase
- Perceived craftsmanship and care
- Low anxiety around pricing and contact

### Aesthetic Direction
Luxury-forward visual language with strong craft cues and restrained performance accents.
Dark + metallic palette is acceptable when it remains elegant, readable, and conversion-oriented.

### Design Principles
1. Conversion clarity first: quote and phone/WhatsApp pathways must be obvious and low-friction.
2. Trust must be provable: avoid placeholder or ambiguous information in legal/contact flows.
3. Premium through restraint: fewer decorative effects, stronger hierarchy, higher content confidence.
4. Mobile-first decisions: thumb reach, readability, and concise decision points.
5. Accessibility is part of luxury: clear contrast, larger interactive targets, and explicit feedback states.
